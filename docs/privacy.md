# Privacy policy

This project is a personal tool that makes short videos and uploads them to one YouTube channel that its owner controls. It is not offered to the public and has no other users.

## What it accesses

The tool asks for one Google permission, `https://www.googleapis.com/auth/youtube.upload`. It uses that permission only to upload videos to the owner's own channel. It does not read the channel, its videos, comments, analytics or any other Google account data.

## What it stores

The owner's OAuth credentials are kept as encrypted secrets in the project's GitHub repository. They are used only while an upload runs and are never written to logs or to the repository.

## What it shares

Nothing. The tool collects no data about viewers or other people, uses no analytics or advertising, and sends no data to third parties other than YouTube, as part of the upload itself.

## Revoking access

The owner can remove the tool's access at any time under Third-party access in their Google account settings.
